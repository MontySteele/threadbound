# Threadbound — M1 Writing Pass

Witness lines are drawn randomly per pool, no repeats within a run. The Witness holds the sarcasm monopoly; all other text below stays somber-wry, no sarcasm.

## 2. Event: The Cold Lantern (standard)

**Scene prose:**

The lantern stands upright in the silt, as if set down by someone who meant to return. Its glass is unbroken. Its flame burns a pale, steady blue — and gives off no heat at all. The hand that left it is nearby, still curled in the shape of carrying. Whatever the light is for, it is not warmth. It has waited here a long while, patient as the dark around it, and it does not gutter when you breathe on it.

**Options & results:**

- **Take the lantern** — *The cold climbs your arm and settles somewhere it shouldn't. The light is yours now.* (Gain a card. Lose 6 HP.)
- **Leave it** — *You step back into the dark, and the dark, for once, feels kind.* (Heal 6.)

**Witness comments:**

- *Taken:* "A dead man's light. I'm sure he won't mind. He didn't last time."
- *Left:* "Restraint. In this place. I'll have to recalibrate my contempt."

## 3. Event: The Basin (crossed choice)

**Scene prose:**

The basin is older than the walls around it, stone worn smooth by hands long gone to dust. The water inside is black and perfectly still — too still, as if it has been holding its breath since the Undercroft was sealed. It smells of iron and deep earth. Old script around the rim has been deliberately chiseled away, save for one word: *mend*. One of you may drink. The other decides.

*(Crossed choice: you choose whether your partner drinks.)*

**Options & results:**

- **They drink** — *The water goes down cold and keeps going. Their wounds close. Something in the thread pulls loose.* (Partner heals 12, but begins the next combat Frayed.)
- **They do not drink** — *You turn them from the basin. The black water does not ripple. It can wait.* (Nothing happens.)

**Witness comments:**

- *They drink:* "You fed your partner the mystery water. They'll thank you. Through gritted teeth."
- *They do not drink:* "You denied them the healing basin. Bold. Do explain it to their face."

## 4. Enemy Flavor (somber, no sarcasm)

- **Cinder Husk** — What remains when a fire is given a body and the body is given nothing else.
- **Tallow Wisp** — A votive flame that outlived its prayer and now searches for someone to grieve.
- **Thread-Leech** — It does not want your blood; it wants the thing that binds you, and it is patient.
- **Reliquary Mite** — Small enough to live inside a saint's knucklebone, and old enough to have eaten the saint.
- **Sexton of the Undercroft** — He still digs the graves, though no one has died here in a hundred years who stayed dead.
- **The Mourner** *(elite)* — It weeps for every soul that fought alone, and it will make mourners of you both if you give it the chance.
