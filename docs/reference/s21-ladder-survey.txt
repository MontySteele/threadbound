THE THREE DELIVERABLES (Part 3, read before ruling):

1. THE CURVE. The S18-era cliff (A2 at ~17) is NOT real on this canon —
   A2 reads 43–51; the whole ladder floated up with the S20 canon flip,
   as OQ#66 suspected it might. A2 is the ladder's first real step
   (−13 to −21 pts from A1) — a wall, not a cliff. The ladder is NOT
   monotone: A2→A3 rises on every pairing (below), and A3→A4 is flat
   everywhere (±0.6).

2. THE A3 QUESTION: the inversion is REAL and now UNIVERSAL. A3 reads
   ABOVE A2 on all three pairings — vv 50.6→54.2 (+3.6), vb 43.4→45.6
   (+2.2), bb 43.4→46.6 (+3.2). Same sign on three paired-seed
   comparisons; knot fights/run rises in step (3.17→3.18 / 3.38→3.45 /
   3.43→3.52): the extra elite is fought, and its crossing arrives with
   the S16-D3 pick rewards (plus relic, covet charge, gold) attached —
   the OQ#66 mechanism, confirmed on the shipped canon. Per-seed paired
   read in the S21-STATUS Part 4 table.

3. THE A4/A5 TAIL (first read on a canon with tracks + the nudge):
   A4 is a NO-OP against bot play — A3→A4 deltas +0.2 / 0.0 / −0.6,
   inside noise on every pairing. This is the known bot blindspot
   (OQ#64: bots don't fray — they husband the shared pool), so the
   pierce-fray rung's teeth are UNREADABLE by this instrument; the A4
   human read stays with the fray-relic pile. REPORTED, no ruling
   demanded. A5 (rest-trim) is the ladder's biggest tooth and monotone:
   −8.6 / −18.6 / −16.6 from A4, act-3 lethality roughly doubling
   (vb 5.8→11.2, bb 5.3→9.8) — rest-starved boards arrive at act 3
   thin. Direction healthy for a final rung; numbers stay PROVISIONAL
   per ascension.ts's own header.
