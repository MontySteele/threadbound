# Threadbound — LAWS.md (the standing laws, consolidated)

One page. Each law cites its origin so a successor — model or human —
can find the source ruling. Laws outrank passes (S18-D5): any pass
that collides with a law STOPS AND REPORTS; it never lands.

## Absolute laws (no exceptions, no clever readings)

1. **The Witness never states falsehoods.** Binds every line it
   speaks: pools, hints, mechanicLines, announces, the ending. A line
   that is true only most of the time is false. (Origin: writing law,
   pre-S8; enforced at every strings sign-off; example catches:
   S19 St-26/29 Ring-discount, S20 St-h1.)
2. **The held reveal is enforced by pacing, never exposition.** The
   mirror sacrament, the cradle, the dawn — nothing explains them
   early; the permitted misreading ("descent to Hell") is deliberate.
   (Lore bible §5b, ratified; governs the Overture, the registers,
   Act 4.)

## Process laws

3. **Enumerate → propose → sign-off** for ALL authored content —
   strings, art, deletions. PROVISIONAL until RATIFIED; nothing
   authors before its row signs; unsigned rows stall and say so.
4. **Instruments before balance.** Sim tooling, telemetry, parity
   banks land before behavior-changing work, hard-sequenced. (S7→S16
   lineage; the S20 flip audit catching a live production bug is the
   standing proof.)
5. **Commit separation:** balance commits ≠ content commits ≠ cleanup
   commits. Golden/bank regens are LOUD, in their own commits, with
   attribution.
6. **Stop-and-report on structural surprise.** Gates that fail
   structurally get an OQ and a designer ruling — never a silent
   resize or re-derivation. (S15 Gate-4 → S16 packet → S18-D4 is the
   model case.)
7. **The OQ ledger is the memory.** Rulings append as dated lines in
   OPEN-QUESTIONS.md; parks are rulings too and carry named re-open
   triggers; the decision queue is kept legible.

## Design laws

8. **S13.2 flat-hex-echo cap:** hook hexAll caps at 2. Buffs petition
   for a different axis, not the cap. (Re-affirmed S18-D5,
   gravebloom.)
9. **No more +1s** on the dead-shape cluster — scalar-immunity is
   proven three ways; shape problems get shape fixes. (S18-6a;
   general principle since S17.)
10. **B6: no mixing art sets.** New art goes through
    scripts/art/prompts.json + post-process or it does not land;
    palette tokens are not art.
11. **The braid is the shipped game.** Gates frame as on-braid
    spread; the lane is archaeology (S15; S20 flip; OQ#65 pending
    deletion).
12. **Human playtest data supersedes bot telemetry bands.** Bot
    reads are instruments, not verdicts; feel-questions file to the
    human pile. (Standing; see the protocol doc's agenda.)
13. **Claims, not authority.** Profile state (ascension, codex
    completion) rides client claims clamped server-side; host-claim
    convention per S16-D6 (ascension, Act 4 access).
14. **Diagnostic ordering:** supply before pool size; legibility
    before numbers.

## Instrument conventions

15. **Bands are tripwires around the shipped canon, not targets**
    (S21-2b). Targets wait for human data.
16. **Anchors re-bank only on ruled instrument events, loudly**
    (S20-R1 convention): the re-bank commit carries the environment
    change, the paired attribution, and the band re-read; excursions
    stop-and-report.
17. **Paired same-seed deltas (S16-R1 form)** are the preferred read
    for attribution; n≥100 paired substitutes for n≥200 pooled on the
    deterministic path.
18. **REPORTED-not-banded** for any read over nondeterministic
    transport (WS/solo batteries) and for provisional surfaces
    (ascension ladder pre-human, the Act 4 leg).
19. **Mode-gating:** solo-experience changes gate on
    `mode === 'solo'` / `state.botSeat`; the co-op fleet stays
    byte-identical, proven per commit (S19 Part 1 pattern).
20. **Practical:** `npm run build` at root before vitest or sims;
    env flags inline before the node invocation; canonical anchors =
    seeds 20001–22000, n=2000 ×3, no env prefix (post-S20 canon).
